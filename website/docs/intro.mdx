# Welcome

**Orbiter** is a native Android app (Kotlin and Jetpack Compose) for a self-hosted
[BookOrbit](https://github.com/bookorbit/bookorbit) library server. It's not a hosted service:
you connect it to a server that you (or someone you trust) runs and controls.

The app supports:

- Sign-in with a username and password, or with OIDC
- Library browsing, search, smart scopes, and collections
- Author and series pages, and book details
- An ebook reader (foliate.js) with themes, highlights, notes, offline dictionaries, and reading
  progress sync, plus a PDF reader and a comic reader
- An audiobook player (Media3/ExoPlayer) with background playback, Chromecast, and Android Auto
- Downloads for offline reading and listening

Use the sidebar to get set up:

## Where Orbiter comes from

Orbiter started as a fork of the community
[BookOrbit Android app](https://github.com/deranjer/bookorbit-android) by deranjer, and has since
gone its own way with a new design and features such as offline dictionaries. If you've used that
app, most of this guide will feel familiar, but the steps here are written for Orbiter.

Orbiter is an independent, unofficial app. It is not made, approved, or endorsed by the BookOrbit
project. Its source is at
[github.com/pranavajk1/bookorbit-android](https://github.com/pranavajk1/bookorbit-android), under
the GNU AGPLv3.
