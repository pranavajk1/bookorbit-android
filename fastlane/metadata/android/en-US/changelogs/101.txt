First release of Orbiter, an unofficial app for your BookOrbit server.

• A new design, with a sign-in that follows what your server allows
• Offline first: books you open stay on the device, and the library works without the server
• Reader: highlights, notes, translate, search and read-aloud, plus offline dictionaries
• Audiobooks: controls in the notification and on the lock screen, and playback that keeps going in the background
• Asks before removing a download
